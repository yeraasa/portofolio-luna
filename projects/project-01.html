Blossom is a flower e-commerce website designed to provide a simple, intuitive, and enjoyable shopping experience. The interface is structured to help users easily explore products without feeling overwhelmed.

Users can browse products through categories, search for specific items, view product details, and add products to their shopping cart. The navigation and layout are designed to make each step of the shopping process clear and easy to follow.

The project focuses on creating a smooth user journey, from discovering products to making a purchase. Each interface element is designed with usability and accessibility in mind, helping users find what they need quickly and interact with the website comfortably.
